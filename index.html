About Navpratap

A practice built on breath first, shape second.

I am Navpratap Singh, a yoga practitioner since 2019. I am studying BSc Yoga Therapy at S-VYASA University, Bengaluru, and have completed a 390-hour yoga instructor course at the Central University of Rajasthan.

I was an international coach at the first World Yogasana Championship in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. My teaching starts with the breath and adapts to your body, your level and your day. You do not need to be flexible to begin.
